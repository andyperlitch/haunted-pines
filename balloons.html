🎈 Halloween Balloon Instructions

Thanks for joining this year’s Haunted Pines Halloween! Your glowing balloon takes about five minutes to set up.

Kits will be dropped off on Wednesday. Haven’t received yours? Text us at (408) 768-8117.

What's in your kit

  • 1 balloon
  • 1 mini LED light
  • 1 cone with a hook and a stick

Setup steps

⚠️ Do not set up until the day of Halloween.

  1. Take the mini LED light and pull out the tab to turn it on.
  2. Place the LED light inside the balloon.
  3. Blow up the balloon and tie it off.
  4. Attach the tied end to the cone and slip it under the hook to hold it in place.
  5. Add the cone to the stick.
  6. Place it as close to the street as you can. We suggest taping it to your mailbox or sticking it into the ground.
