Getting here

There's a decent amount of parking on Twin Pines Drive, north of Tan Oak Drive.

Streets are quiet and residential, so please drive slowly and watch for little ghosts and goblins.

Tips for a great night

  • Look for the red balloons. They mark houses that are ready for trick-or-treaters.
  • Bring a flashlight or glow stick. Some streets are dark.
  • Bring a portable speaker and play some music as your group haunts the neighborhood.
  • Stay on sidewalks and driveways, and be kind to lawns and decorations.
  • Please take your wrappers with you.

Who we are

Just neighbors who love Halloween. Our streets usually get only a handful of trick-or-treaters, so we thought we'd send out an invitation. It's free, we're not selling anything, and everyone is welcome.
